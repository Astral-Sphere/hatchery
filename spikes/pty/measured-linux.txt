VERDICT:  backpressure is real and the ceiling is small: 4095 bytes sat in the kernel and the child blocked. It also means a stalled consumer FREEZES the child — the reader must never stop draining while a tool runs, or the timeout kill fires on a child that was merely waiting for us.
